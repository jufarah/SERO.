Where your support goes

i.

The platform

Hosting, the website, the tools that let SERO. exist as an independent space online. Free for everyone to enter.

ii.

The artists

Featuring, crediting, and where possible, paying the people whose work shapes each monthly theme and annual edition.

iii.

The edition

The annual printed magazine — a curated record of a year of themes, voices, and reflections.

A few questions

Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Patronage is month to month. You can pause or cancel at any point — no questions asked.
What do I receive as a Patron?
The monthly curator's letter, founding member recognition, your name in the credits, and early access to each new monthly theme before it goes live for everyone else.
Can I support SERO. in another way?
Yes, and thank you. For one-off gifts, partnerships, or offering your time and skills, email us at info@sero-collective.com.
Is my contribution tax deductible?
SERO. is currently an independent platform, not a registered charity. Contributions are a form of patronage, not a donation in the charitable-relief sense.
I can't contribute right now. Is that okay?
Of course. SERO. is free for everyone, always. You can read every theme, submit your own work, and be fully part of this community without paying anything.

Whether you support us with a contribution or simply by being here — thank you.
