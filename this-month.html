This month's theme
Welcoming Guest

The art of welcome

Long before borders and labels, people understood this much: dignity comes first.

Food, water, warmth — not as politeness, but as protection.

Some customs fade. Some truths remain.

You are welcome here.

001
Before Questions Were Asked
Photography
SERO. · Welcoming Guest
The first gesture of welcome is rarely a word. It is a cup filled before you have explained yourself. Tea poured for a stranger is a quiet sentence — you may stay a while.
002
Across Cultures and Centuries
Painting · Archive
SERO. · Welcoming Guest
The guest at the table is one of the oldest images in human art. Different lands, different dress, different centuries — and always the same scene. Food shared. A place made. The stranger drawn in.
003
The Same Instinct
Photography
SERO. · Welcoming Guest
Different lands. Different rituals. The same instinct. To prepare more than you need, in case someone arrives. To lay a table that says — there is room for you here.
004
You Are Safe Here
Photography
SERO. · Welcoming Guest
Not to impress, but to protect. A way of saying — you are safe here. The deepest welcome asks nothing in return. It simply keeps watch at the door.

A Place to Arrive
Photography
SERO. · Welcoming Guest
A place to arrive without explanation. To sit with stories, and recognise something familiar. Sometimes welcome is simply a chair left empty, waiting, in case you came.
